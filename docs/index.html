The Pattern

Competence first. Strategy follows.

Most organizations start with AI strategy and then hope that competence catches up. It rarely does. The thing is that the organizations who move fastest just get people building. The strategy gets surprisingly sharp once people have actually touched the thing.

The Proof

F-Secure. Agentic coding to company-wide transformation. Under a year.

I built two training tracks at F-Secure. One for coders, one for business people. 200+ people building real agents. The thing that surprised even me is how the transformation just kind of happened once the competence was there.

The Depth

AI transformation fails in the organization, not in the technology.

The tools work. The models are more than capable. But what actually breaks is the organization itself. The politics, the resistance, the processes nobody wants to touch. I have done this for 25 years. The technology is really never the hard part.
