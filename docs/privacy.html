What data and why

We maintain a business contact register for B2B sales, customer relationship management, and advisory services. This includes:

  • Name and professional title
  • Company and role
  • Business contact information
  • Notes from business meetings and conversations
  • A prospect list based on public professional profiles (LinkedIn)

Legal basis

Legitimate interest (EU GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We maintain business contact records for B2B sales and advisory services. All data subjects are business professionals contacted in their professional capacity.

Data sources

Direct contact (meetings, calls, emails), public professional profiles, and referrals from mutual business contacts.

AI processing

We use AI tools (Anthropic Claude) in our work. When business contact data is processed with AI tools, personal data is pseudonymised before processing — company names and person names are reduced to coded abbreviations. No identifiable personal data is transferred to AI service providers.

Disclosures and transfers

Personal data is not disclosed to third parties for their own purposes. Data is stored in cloud storage (Google). AI processing uses pseudonymised data only.

Retention

Business contact data is retained for the duration of the business relationship and up to 2 years after last contact. Data is reviewed annually.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest

Requests: antti@bosser.consulting

Automated processing

AI tools are used in business operations. Personal data is pseudonymised before AI processing. No automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or significant effects is applied.
