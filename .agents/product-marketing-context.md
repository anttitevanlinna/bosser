# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-22*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** AI agent competence and transformation for organizations.
**What it does:** Bosser helps organizations build AI agent competence through hands-on training (Agents 102) and continuous platform intelligence (Transformation Advisor Engine). The approach: people build real agents first, strategy follows from what they learn.
**Product category:** AI transformation consulting / AI agent training
**Product type:** Professional services (training + advisory + deployed research systems)
**Business model:** Training cohorts (per-person pricing, e.g. €500/person for bootstrap), transformation advisory engagements, deployed advisor engine

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Mid-to-large Nordic enterprises with existing AI strategy but a competence gap. Companies where the board has approved AI initiatives but nobody in the room has built an agent.
**Decision-makers:** CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Digital Transformation leads, CDOs. Sometimes CEO at smaller companies.
**Primary use case:** "We have an AI strategy but can't get traction because our people don't have hands-on agent competence."
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get 20+ people building working agents so the AI strategy is grounded in reality
- Scale agentic transformation from engineering to company-wide without external dependency
- Stay current on the agent platform landscape without dedicating full-time headcount to tracking it

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Organizations have AI strategies but no one who has built an agent. Strategy without competence produces governance of an abstraction.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big consultancies (McKinsey, Accenture) deliver strategy decks, not competence. Junior consultants, no hands-on building.
- AI tool vendors train on their own platform, not your reality.
- E-learning (Coursera, etc.) is generic, not embedded in your organization's data/policies/systems.
- Internal attempts stall because nobody has done it before.
**What it costs them:** Months of strategy without progress. Initiative portfolios that produce slides. Governance frameworks governing something nobody has touched. Competitors who build first pull ahead.
**Emotional tension:** The board is asking for progress. The CTO knows the strategy isn't landing. The gap between what was promised and what's happening is growing.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Few exist with this specific focus on building agent competence before strategy.
**Secondary:** Big consultancies (McKinsey, Accenture, Deloitte) — strategy-first, no hands-on building, junior consultants.
**Indirect:** AI tool vendors (Microsoft, Google training programs) — platform-specific, not your reality. E-learning platforms (Coursera, Udemy) — generic, not embedded.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Done it from the inside (F-Secure transformation, not just advised on it)
- Training uses YOUR data, policies, systems — not toy data
- 25 years of organizational transformation experience — can navigate the politics, not just teach the tools
- You keep everything — full usage/modification rights, no ongoing dependency
- The Transformation Advisor Engine is itself an agent system — the medium is the message
**How we do it differently:** Competence first, strategy follows. People build real agents before anyone writes a governance framework.
**Why that's better:** Strategy informed by hands-on experience lands. Strategy from a deck doesn't.
**Why customers choose us:** Practitioner credibility (did it at F-Secure), organizational depth (25 years of transformation), no lock-in (you keep everything).
