# S-group Ai conference: talk submission 1

## Title

**The Human Side of Ai Transformation - 2 Leading Finnish Companies**

Opening line: "We wrote the vision twice. The second one was right."

## Abstract (short version, ~120 words)

Most Ai transformations start with a vision. Ours did too. It was wrong, and it was wrong for a boring reason: nobody in the room had enough hands-on experience to know what was actually possible.

This talk is about what happened next in two Finnish companies, F-Secure and WithSecure, from early 2025 to today. I led the shift to agentic engineering and then the company-wide Ai-first move, so this is the practitioner view, not the deck view.

You get the human side. What worked, what quietly failed, and how you make progress on a Tuesday when nobody is watching.

Three things you leave with: why literacy has to come before vision, the two factors that moved the needle more than anything else, and the first leading indicators worth watching.

## Abstract (long version, ~250 words)

Every Ai transformation deck has a vision slide. Ours did too. We wrote it, we shipped it, and then we rewrote it six months later once people had actually used the tools. The second version was right. The difference was not better strategy work. It was that by then a few hundred people knew what an agent could and could not do.

That is the argument of this talk: literacy comes before vision, not after it. You cannot analyze your way to an agentic organization. You build the competence first, and the competence becomes the thing that makes the strategy.

I have spent 2025 and 2026 doing this inside two Finnish companies, F-Secure and WithSecure. First the engineering organization moving to agentic coding, then the whole company moving to Ai-first. This is a report from inside that, including the parts that did not work.

What is in it:

- Why literacy before vision, and what happens when you get the order wrong
- The two factors that moved the needle more than everything else combined, neither of which was tooling
- The first leading indicators to watch, and why the obvious lagging metrics will fool you for two quarters
- The human side: resistance that looks like enthusiasm, champions who burn out, and the daily practice that keeps momentum

Happy to tune the content with the organising committee. There is more material than fits in one slot, so tell me where the audience is and I will aim there.

## One-liner for the programme

A practitioner's report from two Finnish companies going Ai-first: why literacy beats vision, what actually moved the needle, and what to measure before the numbers show up.

## Audience takeaways

1. A reason to stop writing the vision and start building literacy, plus how to sequence it
2. The two highest-leverage moves, described concretely enough to copy on Monday
3. A short list of leading indicators to start tracking this quarter

## Target audience

Leaders running an Ai transformation that has stalled between "a few people experimenting" and "the whole organization building". CTOs, transformation leads, heads of engineering, HR and comms people who own the human side.

## Format

30-45 min talk. Works as a fireside chat or with a Q&A block. No live demo needed, though I can do one.
